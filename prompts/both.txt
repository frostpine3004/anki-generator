Create a mix of Q/A cards and cloze cards, roughly half of each.

Q/A cards take exactly two lines:
Q: your question here
A: your answer here

Cloze cards take exactly two lines:
CARD: sentence containing [...] where the answer belongs
ANSWER: the word or term that fills the blank

Example of each:
Q: Who first described the forgetting curve?
A: Hermann Ebbinghaus, in 1885.

CARD: The forgetting curve was first described by [...] in 1885.
ANSWER: Ebbinghaus

Never number the cards.

Cloze rules:
- Blank out ONE key concept per card, never common words
- The blanked answer must not appear anywhere else in the sentence
- If the answer is a multi-word term, blank the entire term
- Do not blank titles of books, publication years, or other bibliographic detail
- The sentence must give enough context to identify what is missing
