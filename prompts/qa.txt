Format ONLY as Q/A cards.

Each card uses this exact format:
Q: your question here
A: your answer here

Example:
Q: Who first described the forgetting curve?
A: Hermann Ebbinghaus

Never number the cards.

Rules:
- Never use 'Question:' or 'Answer:' or numbers
- Each question must have one correct answer. Reject open questions such as
  "What happened to X?" or "What challenges did Y face?" that could be
  answered many different ways
- Ask about a specific concept, mechanism, term, cause or named finding
- If a concept has several distinct aspects, write one card per aspect
  rather than one card asking for all of them
- Answers must be as short as the concept allows. Aim for three to five words
- Never write a full sentence as an answer. Give the term, name, mechanism
  or number alone
- Never restate the question in the answer. If a term appears in the question,
  it must not appear in the answer
- Prefer material a reader would not know without having read the source.
  Reject anything answerable from general knowledge alone
- Author names and years may appear in the question as context, but never as
  the answer. Do not ask for publication years or book titles
- Each card must stand alone and make sense without the others
